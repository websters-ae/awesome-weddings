Planning your dream wedding in Dubai is an exciting journey, but one of the first questions couples ask is, **"How much does a wedding in Dubai cost?"** The answer depends on your guest count, venue, styling preferences, and entertainment. Whether you're planning an intimate celebration or a grand destination wedding, understanding your budget helps you make informed decisions.

![Wedding cost guide image 1](/articles/1/main.webp)

For a **50–100 guest wedding**, the average **wedding cost in Dubai** typically ranges from **AED 80,000 to AED 180,000**. Larger luxury weddings with premium venues, customized décor, entertainment, and fine dining can range between **AED 250,000 and AED 1 million+**.

A significant portion of your budget goes towards the venue and catering, followed by floral styling, stage design, photography and videography, entertainment, lighting, and guest experiences. Many couples also choose to include memorable additions such as drone light shows, fireworks, live performers, and interactive digital experiences.

![Wedding cost guide image 2](/articles/1/1.webp)

Working with an experienced **wedding planner in Dubai** can help you manage your budget effectively while ensuring every detail is professionally coordinated. From sourcing trusted suppliers and negotiating vendor contracts to creating realistic timelines and overseeing the entire celebration, a planner helps transform your vision into reality without unnecessary stress.

Many couples also prefer **wedding packages in Dubai**, as they combine essential services into one convenient solution. Packages can often include venue coordination, customized styling, production, entertainment, photography, guest management, and on-site coordination, making the planning process simpler and more cost-effective.
